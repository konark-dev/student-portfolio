# Research

## 1. Research Objective

Understand the problem deeply before deciding the solution or feature
set.

## 2. Research Questions

-   What solutions already exist?
-   How is the problem currently handled?
-   What are the major limitations of existing solutions?
-   What data is available?
-   What technologies are already being used?
-   What are the important user pain points?
-   What constraints exist in real-world deployment?
-   What would make our solution meaningfully better?
