---- What We Learned

Existing platforms already provide professional profiles, developer portfolios, and portfolio-building tools.

Students can use different platforms to showcase different parts of their academic and professional journey.

For our V1, we will focus on bringing the important student information into one simple digital profile.

---- V1 Direction

The platform will allow students to create and maintain a professional digital profile containing:

- Personal information
- Education
- Skills
- Projects
- Certifications
- Achievements
- Professional links

The profile should present this information clearly and provide an easy way to share it.

---- V1 Goal

The goal of V1 is to build a working student-profile platform while practising:

- Team collaboration
- Git/GitHub workflow
- Frontend development
- Backend development
- Database integration
- AI integration
- App development
- UI/UX
- Testing
- Documentation
- Demo preparation

----Future Expansion

Additional features can be considered after the basic V1 workflow is working.

Examples:

- AI assistance
- Recommendations
- Analytics
- Advanced profile customization
- More integrations
- Additional student/career features
